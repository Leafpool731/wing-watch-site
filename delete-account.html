Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your Wing Watch account and the data linked to it at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@wing-watch.com with the subject "Delete my Wing Watch account".
  2. Send it from the email address you signed up with, so we know the account is yours. If you can't, tell us that address and we'll confirm it with you.
  3. We'll delete it within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

Your account (email address and sign-in details), every report you posted, your flags on other reports, and the bug reports you sent. We don't keep any of it afterwards.

No account?

If you only used Wing Watch without signing up, you can delete your reports yourself in the app under My Reports. Uninstalling the app removes its anonymous ID from your phone.
