Privacy policy

Your data and Wing Watch

Wing Watch collects only what it needs to put bird reports on a map. We don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

Effective November 20, 2026. This policy covers the Wing Watch app for Android, the Wing Watch Admin moderation app, and this website.

What we collect

  • Reports you post: the bird type, what happened, the address or place, your notes, and when it happened. Reports are public: everyone using the app can see them. Each report is linked to your account (or your device's anonymous ID), but other users only see "Wing Watch member" or "Anonymous", never your email.
  • An anonymous ID: the first time you open the app, it gets a random ID so you can post reports and later edit or delete them without making an account.
  • Account details, if you create an account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider; we can't see them.
  • Your location, only when you ask: when you tap "use my location" on New Report or the Route Planner, the app reads your phone's location to find your address or plan a walking route. While you follow a route, it keeps updating your position on the map. The app only uses location while it's open, never in the background. We don't store your coordinates; only the address you choose to put in a report is saved.
  • Bug reports you send: your description, your phone's model and Android version, the app version, and your email (or "anonymous").
  • Flags: if you flag a report as inappropriate, we record that your account or anonymous ID flagged it, and any reason you give.

How we use it

  • To show reports on the map, in the Recent list, and in My Reports.
  • To let you sign in, and to edit or delete your own reports.
  • To look up addresses and plan walking routes.
  • To keep the app safe: rate limits, content checks, and blocking accounts that post abuse.
  • To fix bugs you report.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers. It passes through these services, only to make the app work:

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in, and stores everything listed above.
  • Geoapify receives addresses you type or the coordinates from "use my location", to suggest addresses and plan walking routes. Requests go through our server, not straight from your phone.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) receives addresses you type, to check they're real places.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers and unpkg serve the map images and map code, so like any website they see your IP address when the map loads.

Wing Watch's moderators (a small group of team members) can see which account posted or flagged a report, so they can remove abuse and block accounts that break the rules.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage, and our database only lets each account change its own reports.

How long we keep it

Reports, flags, and bug reports stay until you delete them or delete your account. Account details stay until you delete your account.

Your choices

  • You can use Wing Watch without an account.
  • You can edit or delete your reports in the app under My Reports.
  • You can turn off location access in your phone's settings at any time and type addresses instead.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data. See Delete your account.

Children

Wing Watch is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Wing Watch Admin

Wing Watch Admin is a separate app used only by our moderators; it isn't available to the public. It uses the same database as Wing Watch.

  • What it collects: a moderator's email address and password, to sign in (passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider).
  • What it records: each moderation action (removing a report, or muting or banning an account), with which moderator did it and when, so actions can be reviewed.
  • What it shows moderators: reports, flags, and the account linked to each, as described above.
  • It doesn't use location, ads, or tracking. Moderators can ask for their account to be deleted the same way as anyone else: see Delete your account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
