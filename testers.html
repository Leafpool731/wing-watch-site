Android testers wanted

Help us launch Wing Watch

Before Google lets a new app onto the Play Store, at least 12 people have to test it for 14 days. That's where you come in. It's free, there are no ads, and it takes about two minutes to set up.

How it works

  1. Join the tester group. Use the Google account that's signed in to the Play Store on your Android phone.
  2. Wait for the install link. Once the test is live, we'll send everyone in the group a Google Play link to install Wing Watch.
  3. Keep it installed for 14 days. This is the important part. Google counts testers who stay opted in the whole time.
  4. Try it out and tell us what you think. Check the map, plan a route, post a report if you get swooped. Anything broken or confusing, let us know.

Ready?

You'll need an Android phone and a Google account.

Questions

Do I need an iPhone version? Not yet. This test is Android only.

Will anyone see my email? No. Only the Wing Watch team can see the group's member list, and the app never shows your email to other users.

What data does the app collect? See our privacy policy.

Something not working? Use Report a Bug on the app's Account page, or email support@wing-watch.com.
